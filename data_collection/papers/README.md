# Research Papers

This directory contains references to research papers used for validation and background research.

## Excluded from Repository

Due to copyright restrictions, PDF files are **NOT included** in this repository distribution.

## Referenced Papers

The following papers were consulted during development:

1. **Electromagnetic Compatibility References**
   - Paul, C. R. (2006). Introduction to Electromagnetic Compatibility (2nd ed.)
   - Schulz, R. B., Plantz, V. C., & Brush, D. R. (1988). Shielding theory and practice. IEEE Trans on EMC, 30(3), 187-201.

2. **Composite Materials Research**
   - Chung, D. D. L. (2001). Electromagnetic interference shielding effectiveness of carbon materials
   - Al-Saleh, M. H., & Sundararaj, U. (2009). EMI shielding mechanisms of CNT/polymer composites

3. **Material Properties**
   - Mayadas, A. F., & Shatzkes, M. (1970). Electrical-resistivity model for polycrystalline films
   - Matula, R. A. (1979). Electrical resistivity of copper, gold, palladium, and silver

## How to Obtain Papers

All referenced papers are publicly available through:
- **IEEE Xplore**: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/
- **ScienceDirect**: https://www.sciencedirect.com/
- **MDPI**: https://www.mdpi.com/
- **APS Physical Review**: https://journals.aps.org/

Many are also available through institutional libraries or ResearchGate.

## Data Extraction

The `data_collection/` module contains code for extracting EMI shielding data from research papers, but requires you to obtain the PDFs yourself following copyright laws and publisher policies.
